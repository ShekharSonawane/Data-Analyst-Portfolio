### 3. Power Apps - Manufacturing Operations Trackers

A collection of Power Apps developed for manufacturing and operations-related data tracking.

#### Applications Included

**RM Tracker**

Used for tracking raw material inward and outward transactions and maintaining up-to-date material information.

**MP Tracker**

Used for daily manpower attendance tracking and reducing manual Excel-based data entry.

**EGA Tracker**

Used for tracking Excess Give Away (EGA) for finished-goods packets and generating date-wise and shift-wise reports.
